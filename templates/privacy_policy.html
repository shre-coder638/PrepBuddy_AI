PrepBuddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PrepBuddy is a college project designed to help patients receive reminders and information related to preparation for scheduled medical procedures.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how PrepBuddy is used, the service may process information such as:

  • Patient name
  • Phone number
  • Scheduled procedure information
  • Procedure preparation instructions
  • Reminder and confirmation information
  • Messages sent to the PrepBuddy WhatsApp service

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by PrepBuddy is used to provide preparation reminders, receive confirmation messages, and support communication related to procedure preparation.

3. WhatsApp Communication

PrepBuddy may use the WhatsApp Business Platform to send preparation reminders and receive messages from users. WhatsApp processes messages according to its own privacy policies and terms.

4. Sharing of Information

PrepBuddy does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by services required to operate the application, such as hosting, database, messaging, and communication services.

5. Medical Information

PrepBuddy is intended to communicate preparation instructions provided by the relevant healthcare provider or protocol. It is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or emergency medical care.

Users should contact their healthcare provider directly when they have medical questions, concerns, or emergencies.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed by the application. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain application records, or meet applicable requirements.

8. User Requests

Users may contact the PrepBuddy project team regarding questions about their information or its use.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the PrepBuddy application or its data practices change.

10. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the PrepBuddy project team through the contact information provided with the application.
